About the Delta's

River delta's are dynamic landscapes where rivers meet the sea, creating fertile environments and unique ecosystems. In the Americas, the delta's of the Mississippi River and the Paraná River are especially important, shaping local environments, supporting wildlife, and sustaining communities.
